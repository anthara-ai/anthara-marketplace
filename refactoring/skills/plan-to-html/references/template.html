Why in this order

The seams come first because the characterisation tests need somewhere to attach. The tests come before the refactorings they protect, so that every later step has something that goes red when behaviour changes. The public surface comes last, because it is the only part callers can see, and every caller has to be ready before it moves. {{one sentence on the specific steps that run blind and why they are safe to do that way}}
